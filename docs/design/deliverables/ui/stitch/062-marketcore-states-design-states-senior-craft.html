States

The shapes a screen takes before and instead of data — including the marketplace's own: a search that found nothing, a catalog with nothing published, a buyer who has bought nothing, and an order that cannot yet be paid. Empty and error are designed, not defaulted, preserving structural geometry across layout boundaries.
