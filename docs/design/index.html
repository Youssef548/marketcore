A · Ruled ledgerweight + rules

No boxes. Hierarchy comes from weight, hairlines and aligned figures, so the eye reads down the price and stock columns. The primary action is ink rather than a hue, which leaves exactly one colour on the page meaning “attention”. Sharp corners.

B · Control roomrail + panels

A dark tenant rail, a status strip of live counts, and bordered panels on a tinted canvas. Navigation is spatial: the tenant is always on the left, the work is always to its right. Dense rows, small radii, one signal amber.

C · Quiet catalogair + type

Air instead of grid. Larger type, softer surfaces, one rule between rows instead of a cell lattice, and a fuller touch target. Readable before it is dense — the bet is that a catalog is read more often than it is scanned at speed.

D · Master & detailB + a detail pane

B's rail, with the row you select opening beside it instead of on another page. The list answers “which one”; the pane answers “what is true about it, and what do I do”. Same palette as B on purpose, so what you are judging is the structure.

E · Dark consoleB, lights off

B's structure on a dark canvas, for a screen that is open all day. The palette is chosen rather than inverted: on a dark surface the legible range for small text is bounded from below, so the accent becomes a light fill with dark text on it. Not a terminal — proportional type, real panels, no glow.

F · Command barsearch leads

The rail is gone, search is the widest thing on the page, counts move to a footer, and row density becomes a control rather than a decision made for you. Compacts to 28px rows. Try the Comfortable / Compact toggle — it works in this mockup.

Tokens

switches with the direction

Every value is a variable; no component hard-codes a colour. Contrast is computed from the tokens below with the standard OKLCH → sRGB conversion, so a token edit that breaks legibility reports itself here instead of waiting for review.

Type, space, corners

one family, three densities

Controls, in their states

idle · hover · focus · busy · disabled

Focus is :focus-visible and drawn as a 2px ring at 2px offset, never removed. State is never carried by colour alone: a dot, a word, or a shape comes with it.

Sign in

decide · one action

The one surface allowed to be centred: the visitor has a single job and no context to preserve. The refusal below is the API's own message, so what a person reads here is what the server said.

Products are the API's real surface today, so this screen is the app's actual destination rather than an invention. Orders and payouts are shown in the rail as planned, labelled with the phase that will bring them — a nav item that pretends to work is the kind of thing this repository refuses.

The app, in each option

catalog · sign in

The product rather than a component sheet: the tenant rail, the top bar, the counts, the six-product table with a name long enough to truncate and a published product at zero stock, and the sign-in screen. Every option renders identical markup and data, so the only thing that changes is the design system. Each one is drawn for this product — a platform that holds physical stock, seeded with Egyptian cotton textiles — rather than borrowed from another company's identity.
